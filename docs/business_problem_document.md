# Customer Shopping Behavior & Repeat Purchase Analysis

## Business Context

UrbanNest is a mid-sized D2C home and lifestyle brand.

The company has observed that customer acquisition is increasing, but repeat purchases are declining.

Management wants to understand customer behavior and identify opportunities to improve retention and revenue.

---

## Business Questions

1. Which customer segments generate the highest revenue?

2. Which customer segments have the highest repeat purchase rate?

3. Which product categories drive repeat purchases?

4. Are there seasonal trends affecting customer purchasing behavior?

---

## Objective

Analyze customer purchasing patterns to identify factors influencing retention, revenue, and customer lifetime value.

---

## Stakeholders

- CEO
- Marketing Director
- E-commerce Manager
- Product Team

---

## Scope

Included:
- Customer data
- Orders
- Products
- Payments
- Time-based sales analysis

Excluded:
- Marketing campaign attribution
- Inventory forecasting
- Website clickstream data

---

## Success Metrics

- Repeat Purchase Rate
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
- Revenue from Repeat Customers
